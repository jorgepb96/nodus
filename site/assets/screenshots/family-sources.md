# Sources of the family screenshots and icons

Screenshots of the other Nodus apps, converted to WebP for the family pages
(`site/imago/`, `site/link/`, `site/reader/`, `site/presenter/`, `site/drift/`
and the front page). Each comes from the app's own repository.

| Folder | Source |
|---|---|
| `imago/` | `jorgepb96/nodus-imago` · `docs/screenshots/readme-*.png` (1280 px wide). The photographs shown are by Jean Laurent and his studio from public collections; rights for each are listed in that repository's `docs/screenshots/README.md`. |
| `link/` | `jorgepb96/nodus-mobile` · `docs/mobile-0.1.0/appstore/screenshots/iphone-en-*.png` (600 px wide). Example vault content. |
| `reader/` | `jorgepb96/nodus-reader` · `design/screens/iPhone-*.png` (600 px wide). Public-domain and published book titles only. |
| `presenter/` | `jorgepb96/nodus-presenter` · `Screenshots/iphone-*.png` and `iphone16promax-{dark-estudio,light-docencia}.png` (600 px wide). Demo presentation. |
| `drift/` | `jorgepb96/nodus-drift` · `screenshots/{01-home,02-mix,03-saved,04-meditation-controls,08-sound-library}.png` (600 px wide). |

App icons in `site/assets/apps/` come from each app's asset catalogue
(`AppIcon`), resized to 256 px: Imago `build/icon.png`, Link
`ios/Nodus/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/icon-1024.png`, Reader
`design/icon/AppIcon-Dark.png`, Presenter
`App/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/icon-dark.png`, Drift
`ios/NodusDrift/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`.
