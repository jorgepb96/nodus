Nodus Reader

Your library, read aloud

Nodus Reader is a free, open-source app for reading and listening to your library. Narration and document processing run on your device, and your library can stay local or sync through your own iCloud.

Free, and yours

Free to use. Yours to keep.

Nodus Reader is open source under the AGPL-3.0-only licence. There is no account: your books stay on your device or in your personal iCloud.
