# Kokoro-82M benchmark — 21 October 2026

Apple M2, macOS, Nodus development build, isolated QA profile. Kokoro-82M int8
(`kokoro-v1.0.int8.onnx`, ONNX Runtime 1.31.0, `kokoro-onnx` 0.6.1) on the CPU.
Both texts are 9.5 s of speech; the clip was generated by `ttsSpeak` with
`save` and the real-time factor is the service's `genS / audioS`.

| Run | Voice | Audio | First fragment | Generation | RTF |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Spanish, cold | `kokoro:ef_dora` | 9.60 s | 2.86 s | 1.70 s | 0.177 |
| English, warm | `kokoro:af_heart` | 9.49 s | 1.39 s | 1.37 s | 0.144 |
| Spanish, warm | `kokoro:ef_dora` | 9.60 s | 1.39 s | 1.38 s | 0.143 |
| English, warm | `kokoro:af_heart` | 9.49 s | 1.34 s | 1.32 s | 0.139 |

The first fragment includes the model load; warm runs are about 6.9–7.2× real time.
Both clips were transcribed back with Whisper and matched the input text. After
removing and reinstalling the Kokoro runtime, a 4.33 s Spanish clip generated in
0.98 s (RTF 0.227, first fragment 2.15 s including load).

Runtime install: ~8 s (155 MB). Model + voice pack download: ~4 s (120,575,669 bytes).
Model delete and re-download, and runtime remove and reinstall, were exercised from the
real app and leave the same status shape as Ming and Phonon.
