# Bundled reference voices

## es-dave (Español de España)

- `es-dave.wav` / `es-dave.txt`: a 9.8 s clip (`0000000003`, "General" set) from the
  `es_ES/dave` voice dataset, recorded by its author for text-to-speech voices.
- Source: https://github.com/OHF-Voice/voice-datasets (release v1.0.0, `es_ES-dave.zip`).
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain). Nodus credits the dataset as its authors ask.
- Converted to 44.1 kHz mono PCM16; the audio content is unchanged.
