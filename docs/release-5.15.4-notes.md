## Fixes

- The Listen panel shows voice engine installation phases and progress, lets you cancel the installation and displays errors. Model downloads show activity as soon as you click the button. Opening the panel refreshes its status, and models that require accepting terms take you to their section in Settings.

- In Nodus Scriptor, the Listen button in the header only opens or closes the panel. Opening it no longer plays or generates audio automatically. Playback, generation and reading selected text start from their controls.
