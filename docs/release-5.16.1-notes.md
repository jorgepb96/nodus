## Enhancements

- Settings groups its sections in a side navigation. AI models brings chat and text, extraction, vision and OCR, embeddings and reranking models together in one card. Tabs separate semantic search, local models, images, transcription, narration and advanced options. The basic/advanced switch is removed. Each tool keeps its model picker, and changing the main model preserves independent choices.

- In Ideas and Authors, the Filters button opens a floating panel below the button without moving the catalog. Ideas groups Type and Order. Authors groups Saved authors, Synthesis and Order. The panel closes when you click the button again, click outside or press Escape.
