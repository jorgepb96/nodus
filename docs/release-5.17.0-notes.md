# Nodus 5.17.0

## New features

- New Podcast section, in beta, under Analyze. Pick a topic and Nodus researches it in your vault, writes a script and records it with local voices: Kokoro, Phonon or Ming. There are four formats: conversation, debate, lesson and narrated essay, with one or two voices and from 5 to 30 minutes. You can review and edit the script before recording. The episode keeps playing when you switch sections. It downloads as M4A or WAV, with chapters and a transcript that follows the audio. It is available in Academic, Study and Teaching vaults.

- You can now sign in with Hugging Face in Settings, Voice and narration, instead of pasting a token. Your browser opens, you give permission and you return to Nodus. This lets you download the gated voice models whose terms you have accepted. The session is stored encrypted on this computer and you can sign out at any time. A read token still works as an alternative.
