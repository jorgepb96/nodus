Nodus Drift

A little space to be

Nodus Drift plays ambient sounds for reading, focus and rest. It includes 89 sounds, mixes of up to six, and works without a network connection.

Join the TestFlight beta Source code

iPhone · iPad · iOS 17 or later

There are no accounts, ads, tracking, subscriptions or purchases. Audio is decoded, mixed and played on the device.
